# Demo video — voiceover

Read calmly. Pause where the line breaks. Each block lines up with what's on screen.

---

**[Onboarding: /start, name, timezone, sun sign — 0:00–0:25]**

This is Computer Josiyam. Josiyam is Tamil for astrology.

Setup takes three taps: my name, my timezone, my sun sign. Then I paste a calendar link, once. Today I'm using a sample day, and I'll say that plainly. The bot's first message says it too.

After this, I never type anything it needs.

**[/play → day reading — 0:25–0:40]**

Every morning, a reading of the day ahead. Not a to-do list. Just a look at the shape of it.

**[⏰ standup, 👍 — 0:40–0:50]**

Before each meeting, one line. In one of four voices: astrology, hype, warm, or a quote. A thumbs-up teaches it which voice lands.

**[✅ standup, 😐, note — 0:50–1:05]**

After each meeting: how did it go. One tap. Or a line of my own, if I want. That's my journal, and I never had to open one.

**[⏰ investor call, 👎 — 1:05–1:15]**

This one's an investor call. The bot knows it's a hard meeting from the title, and steadies rather than cheers. Thumbs-down — and the next one will change register.

**[✅ investor call, 🔥, note — 1:15–1:30]**

It went well. I say so. Watch what happens next.

**[⏰ 1:1 with Priya — 1:30–1:40]**

The next nudge already knows what I wrote. It read the journal.

**[🌙 day closed — 1:40–1:55]**

At nine, the day closed. Every meeting, my mood for each, my notes, and one reflection.

**[📊 stats — 1:55–2:05]**

Everything it learned is here. Which voices I like, how fast I react, how my days go.

Nothing was asked of me except a tap.
